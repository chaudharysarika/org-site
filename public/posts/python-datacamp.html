What is statistics?

  • The practice and study of collecting and analysing data.
  • A summary statistics is a fact about or summary of some data.

What can statistics do?

  • How likely someone is to purchase a product? Are people more likely to purchase it if they can use a different payment system?
  • How many occupant your hotel have? How can you optimise occupancy?
  • How many sizes of jeans need to be manufactured so that they can fit 95% of the population? Should the same number of each size be produced?
  • A/B tests: which add is more effective in getting people to purchase a product?

What statistics can’t do?

  • Why is Game of Thrones so

Types of statistics

  • Descriptive Statistics
    • Describe and summarise
  • Inferential Statistics
    • use sample data to make inferences about a larger population

Two Types of Data

  • Numeric (Quantitative)

    • Discrete Data
  • Categorical Data (Qualitative data)

    • Ordinal
